## How Team Discussions Work

**When to use**:
- 2+ agents raise the same concern about an approach
- Technical decision requires multiple perspectives
- Scope or priority dispute
- Major architectural decision

**Process**:
1. Agent raises concern in their status file or blockers file
2. Jeff sees pattern (2+ agents concerned)
3. Jeff opens discussion here with clear question
4. All relevant agents provide perspective
5. Team reaches consensus
6. Jeff makes final call if needed
7. Decision documented, everyone moves forward

**Format**:
```
### [DISCUSSION-001] Brief title
**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Participants**: @Agent1, @Agent2, @Agent3
**Question**: What are we deciding?

**@Agent1 perspective**:
[Agent's view]

**@Agent2 perspective**:
[Agent's view]

**@Agent3 perspective**:
[Agent's view]

**Decision**:
[Final decision reached]

**Rationale**:
[Why this decision was made]

**Status**: RESOLVED
```

---

**Example Usage**:

Let's say both Callie and Aphex disagree on file cleanup strategy:
- Callie wants time-based cleanup (delete after 1 hour)
- Aphex wants session-based cleanup (keep until user leaves)

Jeff would open a discussion here, both provide their reasoning, team discusses trade-offs, reaches consensus. Decision is documented, everyone implements consistently.

---

**Trust and respect**: All agents are god-tier experts. Disagreements are opportunities to find the best solution. Everyone's perspective is valued.
